Founded in 2018 by Chef Antoine Laurent following his tenure at three-star establishments across Lyon and San Francisco, Savoria was born out of a desire to strip away pretension while elevating flavor to its purest essence.

We partner directly with regional bio-dynamic farmers, artisanal foragers, and coastal day-boat fishermen. Every dish is a dialogue between timeless French technique and progressive culinary innovation.

"Great dining is not simply food — it is an emotional voyage, a tactile memory that lingers long after the final glass is emptied."

— Antoine Laurent, Executive Chef & Founder

Dining Guidelines & Etiquette

  • Dress Code: Smart Casual to Elegant. We kindly ask gentlemen to avoid flip-flops and athletic wear.
  • Grace Period: Tables are held for 15 minutes past your reserved time before release.
  • Complimentary Valet: Available at the front portico Tuesday through Sunday from 5:00 PM onwards.
  • Cancellations: No fees apply if cancelled up to 6 hours before dinner seating.
